Engineering Methodology

Our Rigorous CFD Workflow

Every project follows a validated 5-stage simulation protocol to ensure physical realism, mesh independence, and seamless authority clearance.

01

CAD Simplification

Cleaning complex architectural Revit/AutoCAD/SolidWorks models, extracting the fluid domain, and sealing leakage boundaries.

02

High-Fidelity Meshing

Generating structured polyhedral and boundary layer prism grids with y+ wall spacing to resolve near-wall velocity gradients.

03

Physics & Boundary Setup

Configuring turbulence models (k-ε, SST k-ω, LES), radiation (DO), combustion rates, and thermal boundary conditions.

04

HPC Cluster Execution

Solving Navier-Stokes equations across high-performance multi-core computing nodes until strict convergence criteria are met.

05

Compliance Reporting

Delivering comprehensive 3D vector plots, ISO-surfaces, tenability timeline graphs, and stamped authority submission reports.
